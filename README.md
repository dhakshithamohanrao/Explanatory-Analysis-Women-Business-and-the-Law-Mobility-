# Women, Business and the Law (WBL): Women's Mobility Rights – Data Visualisation & Analysis

## Project Overview

This project explores gender-based legal barriers affecting women's freedom of movement across the world using the World Bank's **Women, Business and the Law (WBL) – Mobility** dataset.

The analysis examines regional disparities, global reform trends, passport restrictions, residential autonomy, nationality rights, and the relationship between national income levels and women's legal mobility rights.

The objective is to transform legal and economic indicators into meaningful visual insights that can support policymakers, human rights organisations, and international development institutions in identifying areas requiring legislative reform.

## Objectives

- Analyse global and regional differences in women's legal mobility rights.
- Identify economies with significant restrictions on women's freedom of movement.
- Examine changes in mobility rights and legislative reforms over time.
- Explore the relationship between income levels and legal equality.
- Communicate findings through effective data visualisations and evidence-based recommendations.

## Dataset

- **Source:** World Bank – Women, Business and the Law (WBL)
- **Topic:** Mobility
- **Dataset indicator:** `GD_WBL_MOB`
- **Reference year:** 2024 for the main comparative analysis
- **Geographical coverage:** Global economies and regional aggregates.

## Key Areas of Analysis & Visualisations

The project explores the following themes through multiple visualisation techniques:

1. **Regional Mobility Divide:** Compares average legal mobility scores across global regions.
2. **Global Reform Trends:** Examines changes in global mobility scores over time.
3. **The Passport Gap:** Highlights the distribution of economies with and without full legal mobility rights.
4. **Residential Autonomy:** Examines women's legal right to choose where they live.
5. **Generational Mobility:** Explores gender equality in nationality and citizenship rights.
6. **Wealth and Mobility:** Compares mobility scores across national income groups.
7. **Legislative Reform Progress:** Highlights selected countries that have made significant legal reforms.
8. **Regional Disparities:** Identifies the highest- and lowest-scoring regions.
9. **Historical Regional Trends:** Uses a heatmap to examine the evolution of mobility rights.
10. **Global Mobility Profile:** Uses radial bar and radar charts to compare regional mobility scores.

## Key Findings

- Significant regional differences remain in women's legal mobility rights.
- The analysis highlights a gap between high-performing regions and regions where legal restrictions remain substantial.
- Global mobility scores show a slower pace of improvement in the later years of the analysed period.
- Passport restrictions and limitations on choosing one's residence remain important areas for legislative reform.
- Income-level comparisons reveal differences in average legal mobility scores across economic groups.
- Selected country examples demonstrate that substantial legislative improvements are possible.

## Recommendations

Based on the visual analysis, the project proposes:

- Prioritising legislative reforms addressing passport and travel restrictions.
- Supporting targeted legal advocacy in economies with lower mobility scores.
- Encouraging knowledge-sharing around successful legislative reforms.
- Promoting gender-sensitive policies through international development initiatives.

## Limitations

- The dataset measures legal rights (de jure) rather than actual implementation or enforcement (de facto).
- National-level aggregates may conceal differences between regions and communities within individual countries.
- Visual comparisons identify patterns and associations but do not establish causal relationships.
